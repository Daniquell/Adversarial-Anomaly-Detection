# Detection of Adversarial Attacks Based on the Analysis of Anomalies in the Internal Representations of the Neural Network

## 📄 Abstract

**Keywords:** `adversarial attacks` `anomaly detection` `internal representations` `convolutional neural network` `model security` `generalizing ability` `hyperparameter optimization` `Fashion-MNIST`

> **Object of the research:**  
> Mechanisms for ensuring the information security of convolutional neural networks in image processing.

> **Aim of the work:**  
> To develop and experimentally study a method for detecting adversarial attacks based on the analysis of anomalies in the activations of hidden layers of the neural network.

> **Results:**  
> As a result of this work, a comprehensive study was carried out to assess the effectiveness of methods for detecting adversarial attacks based on the analysis of anomalies in the activations of the intermediate layers of convolutional neural networks.  
>  
> A review of modern approaches to the detection of adversarial examples was conducted, with an emphasis on methods that do not require model modification.  
>  
> A reproducible technological pipeline was implemented, including:  
> - generation of adversarial attacks,  
> - extraction of feature representations,  
> - training of the anomaly detector.  
>  
> Comparative testing was performed on four model architectures, including configurations obtained through hyperparameter optimization in a preceding study.
